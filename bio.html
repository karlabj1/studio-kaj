Bio

KAJ (b. 2003, Beirut, Lebanon) works from the infrastructural undercurrents of the city. Raised between Beirut, Antwerp, and living between Paris and New York, he moves fluently through dense, improvised, and highly regulated urban maps. Poles, road-signs, construction materials and fixtures, elements that typically recede into the background are isolated, repeated, and recalibrated across his works, returning as charged sites where questions of access, control, and care are staged rather than resolved.

The environments he constructs propose these ubiquitous objects as anchors, opening a charged contemplative space in which viewers can register their own routes through the city and the subtle rules that organize them.
